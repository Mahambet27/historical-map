# 2–3 minute demonstration

**0:00 — Problem.** Historical learning is often split between a static textbook, a separate map
and an archive. Qazaq Heritage Map joins time, place, people and evidence in one educational atlas.

**0:20 — Time.** Start the presentation and show how the whole territory of Kazakhstan is visible.
Move from the Saka period to 552 and explain that every state changes the map, events and evidence.

**0:45 — 1465.** Select the formation of the Kazakh Khanate. Open its card. Point out Kerei,
Janibek, political centres and the permanent warning that the border is a scholarly reconstruction.

**1:15 — Comparison.** Open “Compare years: 1465 ↔ 1511”. Explain that the text is curated in the
dataset; the platform does not invent a historical conclusion from geometry.

**1:40 — Evidence.** Open academic sources. Show the official portal, reviewed scholarship and the
verification status attached to each item.

**2:00 — Educational agent.** Ask “Explain for a Grade 7 student”. The offline assistant selects
the right period and entity while answering only from the reviewed exhibition pack.

**2:25 — Scale.** Close with the Grade 7 mini-lesson and explain how the same architecture can join
published collections from schools, universities, museums and archives through PostGIS and a
review workflow.
